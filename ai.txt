# New Year's Eve - PropFirmDiscount.com

New Year's Eve prop firm deals and the discount codes of the firms that ran them.

## Dataset

40 deals from 25 prop firms, newest 2026-01-22.
Each row states the deal, its dates, the firm's discount code, and where to redeem it.
